About Kurinji Resort

Rooted in the hills, built for rest

Kurinji Resort sits near Pattysery Dam in G.N Puram, Kanthalloor — one of Kerala's coolest, mistiest hill villages. Named after the rare Neelakurinji flower that blankets these hills once every twelve years, the resort is built around the same idea: something worth waiting for.

From cosy Standard rooms to the sprawling two-storey Premium Villa, every stay includes a complimentary breakfast and easy access to the valley's cardamom estates, dam views and cool mountain trails.
