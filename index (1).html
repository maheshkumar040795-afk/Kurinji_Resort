About Kurinji Resort

Rooted in the hills, built for rest

Kurinji Resort sits near Pattysery Dam in G.N Puram, Kanthalloor — one of Kerala's coolest, mistiest hill villages. Named after the rare Neelakurinji flower that blankets these hills once every twelve years, the resort is built around the same idea: something worth waiting for.

From cosy Standard and Quad rooms to the spacious Premium Villas, every stay includes a complimentary breakfast and easy access to the valley's cardamom estates, dam views and cool mountain trails.

Glimpses from the trail

Kanthalloor Sightseeing Locations

Best places to visit in and around Kanthalloor — many covered on our jeep safari routes.

1

Kanthalloor

A scenic hill village known as the "Apple Valley of Kerala" with beautiful fruit orchards, vegetable farms, and cool weather.

2

Irachil Para Water Falls

One of the most popular waterfalls near Kanthalloor, surrounded by rocky hills and greenery.

3

Kanthalloor Waterfalls

A picturesque waterfall ideal for photography and relaxing in nature.

4

Muniyara – Anakottapara Park

An ancient megalithic site with historic stone burial chambers and panoramic hill views.

5

Bhramaram View Point

A famous viewpoint offering breathtaking sunrise, sunset, and valley views.

6

Marayoor Sandalwood Forest

India's only natural sandalwood forest, located about 15 km from Kanthalloor.

7

Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary

A wildlife sanctuary known for elephants, spotted deer, giant squirrels, and trekking trails.

8

Keezhantoor Waterfalls

A peaceful waterfall hidden amidst forests.

9

Apple & Fruit Orchards

Visit local farms growing apples, peaches, plums, strawberries, oranges, and passion fruit.

10

Kanthalloor Tours N Jeep Safari

Enjoy off-road jeep safaris through forests, viewpoints, and plantations.
